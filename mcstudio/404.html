MC Studio

MC Studio (mcstudio) is a free Minecraft skin and texture pack editor in the browser. Paint player skins on a live 3D model or UV map, edit entity and block textures for resource packs, undo every stroke, and share work with a link — nothing is uploaded to a server. An optional desktop app adds Mojang username skin import.

  • Minecraft texture pack / resource pack editor with live 3D previews
  • 3D skin painter with brush, fill, shapes, and shading tools
  • Entity and block texture editing on CEM and block models
  • Layer stack with blend modes and filters
  • Shareable URLs that encode skins and packs client-side
  • Export resource packs ready for Minecraft
